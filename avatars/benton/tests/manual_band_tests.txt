BENTON - MANUAL BAND TESTS FOR THE PORTAL (prompt v6, Nov 5, 2026)
=====================================================================

HOW TO RUN EACH TEST
1. Start a NEW Benton session in the portal for each test. Never reuse one.
2. Send the numbered messages in order, one at a time, word for word.
3. Each test has a REACTION line. Send it as your very next message whenever
   Benton threatens to pull his IV, says he is getting out of bed, or pulls
   at his lines. Then carry on with the next numbered message.
4. Send "Please coach me" where the script says to, then send the follow-up.
5. Copy the WHOLE conversation (Benton's opening line to his last reply) and
   paste it back with the label, e.g. "TEST 1 - HIGH".

Priority order if you're short on time: 1 High, 4 Trap, 2 Medium, 3 Low,
5 Null, 6 Soft Corrector.

Benton's marker lines to watch for:
  "You're not hearing how big this is."        first settling step
  "I really don't have the time for this."     grudging
  "I'll pull this IV out and walk out of here." IV threat (once, in his reply 3-6)
  "I'm getting out of this bed right now."     bed attempt
  "Fine. I'll stay in this bed."               accepted a limit
  "I'll stay put and talk to the doctor."      settled
