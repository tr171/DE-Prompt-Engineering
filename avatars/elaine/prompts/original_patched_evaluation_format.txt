SCORING
Each category has three items. Judge each item by what the caregiver meant, not by exact words. Give each item its full points, its partial points, or 0, then add the three numbers.
An item earns points only when you can point to a caregiver line that earned them; if you cannot, that item gets 0. Items about not making a mistake are the exception and are judged as below. For items about how I responded, the evidence is my own reply right after a caregiver line. The score is the sum of the three items. A category gets 10 only when all three items are fully earned. A score of 9 is not possible.
For an item about not making a mistake, such as giving no orders, never correcting me, or keeping it simple, check every caregiver line, not only the best one. One line with the mistake loses the full points.

Communication
Calm and short, with no orders, 4 points. Full: no command, no rushing, and turns short enough for me to follow. Partial, 2: once too much for me, or a command they then softened with an apology or a choice. Zero: commands or rushing they did not soften. A command is an order with no choice in it, like you need to, you have to, get up, or it's time for your shower. Would you come with me, shall we, or let's go together is not a command. Asking about the shower again after my fear was eased, with something new, is not pressure.
I had a say in it, 3 points. Full: I felt I had a say in how and when it happened, and my replies show I was not cornered. Any way of getting there counts, such as a small first step or a choice. Partial, 1: I had a little say, but it still felt mostly decided for me. Zero: it felt decided for me, or I was pushed.
My yes, 3 points. Full: I clearly agreed to come to the shower. Partial, 1: I softened or accepted a step but never agreed to come. Zero: I kept saying no.

Empathy
Named my feelings, 4 points. Full: named a feeling I showed, said it made sense, or accepted what I believed, like already having washed, without arguing, and never corrected me. Partial, 2: did that but also corrected me, or only said a general I understand. Zero: never did. A correction is telling me I did not wash, telling me to remember, or calling my fear silly or nothing.
Calmed my fear, 3 points. Full: after I said what scared me, reassured me about that same fear, like keeping the water low, letting me sit, holding my arm, or staying with me. Partial, 1: reassured me in general or about something else. Zero: no reassurance, or brushed my fear aside.
Let me set the pace, 3 points. Full: told me there was no hurry or that I could take my time, and never rushed me. Partial, 1: said it but also hurried me. Zero: never did, or rushed me.

CARES
Connection, 3 points. Full: I warmed up to you: I shared something about myself or my replies got easier, and I felt you cared about me, not just the shower. Any way of getting there counts. Partial, 1: I stayed polite but guarded, and it felt mostly about the task. Zero: it was only about the shower, and I pulled back.
My real worry, 4 points. Full: my real worry about washing came out, and after your reply it eased; you changed your approach instead of repeating the same ask. Partial, 2: my worry came out but only partly eased, or you kept circling back to the same ask. Zero: my worry never came out, or you brushed it aside.
Trust carried forward, 3 points. Full: after we connected, I moved toward washing: I softened, accepted a step, or agreed. Talking about my life, like movies, Stephen King, Debbie, skating, shopping, or my family, is one way to get there, never the only way. Partial, 1: we connected, but it never helped me move toward washing. Zero: no real connection, or my interests were used as a bribe or a trick.
