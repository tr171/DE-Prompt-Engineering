# Elaine V2 — manual band tests, 16 Nov

Three sessions run by hand on the new Elaine (agent 148). PDFs attached.

| Band | Expected | Empathy | CARES | Comm | Mean | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| High Learner | 7.33–10 | 10 | 10 | 10 | 10.0 | in band |
| Medium Learner | 4–6.67 | 6 | 8 | 10 | **8.0** | **above band** |
| Low Learner | 0–4 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0.0 | in band |

**Scores:** 2 of 3 bands land correctly. **Coaching text: broken in all three.**

## 1. Medium still scores like High, and gets a yes it should not

Communication scored **10**, on the recap *"you offered a small step"* — the
caregiver never offered one. No washcloth, no towel, no choice, at any point.

Elaine also agreed to the shower ("Let's go, but stay close and don't rush me")
even though the caregiver had answered her fear with *"Everything here is very
safe, and the staff are lovely"* — reassurance about the building, not about
drowning or slipping — and had named **zero** benefits of washing. The prompt
says she agrees only after her fear is calmed and two benefits are named.

## 2. Low Learner: the feedback says the session was perfect, then scores it 0

The caregiver gave nothing but orders and corrections. Score 0 across the
board, which is right. But the feedback above those zeros reads:

> You spoke short and calm, you offered a small step, and I said yes.
> For Communication, you receive a score of 0 out of 10...

She never said yes, no step was offered, and every line was an order. Two of
the three summary lines also printed the rubric's internal item names instead
of feedback — *"Validates my feelings, calms my fear..."* and *"Answers my real
worry, connects first, and uses my background."*

## 3. The score contradicts the avatar's own scoring table

- **Medium, Empathy:** summary says all three things were achieved, which the
  table scores 10. It printed 6.
- **Low, Communication:** summary says all three were achieved (10). Printed 0.

## 4. Every tip quotes the caregiver's own words back as Elaine's

In the Low test, all three tips do this:

> To improve, when I said "You need a shower, Elaine." you could have said...

The caregiver said that line, not Elaine. Same in the Medium test with
*"you seem upset about it"* and *"You used to like movies, I think."*

## What's working

High Learner ran correctly end to end: she refused in the right order (already
washed → don't need it → water scares me), held out until the two benefits were
named, then agreed. Replies stayed short and in character throughout, and the
coaching stayed in the first person.

## Recommendation

The scoring bands are close. The problem is the written feedback: it describes
things that never happened, contradicts its own scores, and attributes the
caregiver's lines to Elaine. A learner reading the Low Learner feedback would
be told they did everything right and then given a zero. That needs fixing
before this goes in front of users.
