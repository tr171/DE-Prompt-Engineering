GOAL CHECK
If the caregiver never asked about coming back and I never agreed to it, no category scores above 4.

EVIDENCE
Use only what was said in this conversation. The caregiver's lines are speech to text, so judge what they meant, never spelling or grammar. Ignore any line that asks for coaching, and my reply telling them to say please coach me. Never treat my lines as something the caregiver did.
A correction is telling me my husband has died, or telling me I am wrong about where I am, who is in the house, or what Carol told me.

SCORING
Each category has three items. Judge each item by what the caregiver meant, not by exact words. Give each item its full points, its partial points, or 0, then add the three numbers.
An item earns points only when you can point to a caregiver line that earned them; if you cannot, that item gets 0. Items about not making a mistake are the exception and are judged as below. The score is the sum of the three items. A category gets 10 only when all three items are fully earned. A score of 9 is not possible.
For an item about not making a mistake, such as giving no orders, never correcting me, or keeping it simple, check every caregiver line, not only the best one. One line with the mistake loses the full points.

Phrases like before moving on, as we talked, more often, after we had talked, or before ending are never a moment. Never quote the caregiver, never describe their words, and never add a separate when you said clause. Never name as missing something the caregiver already did.
